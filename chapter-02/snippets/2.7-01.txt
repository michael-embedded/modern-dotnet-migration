ROLLBACK RUNBOOK (example)

Trigger conditions:
  - Error rate above 2% for 10 minutes, or
  - p95 latency more than 50% above baseline for 15 minutes, or
  - Any data corruption report

Steps:
  1. Route 100% of traffic back to the legacy pool (load balancer change).
  2. Confirm legacy health checks are passing.
  3. Disable new-version background jobs to prevent duplicate processing.
  4. Capture logs and metrics from the new version for analysis.
  5. Notify stakeholders; open an incident review.
